# Project Charter

# E-Commerce Product Growth & Causal Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project aims to analyze large-scale e-commerce clickstream data (~68 million user events) to identify the root causes behind revenue stagnation despite increasing traffic.

The project simulates the role of a Product Data Analyst working closely with Product Managers and Business Stakeholders to investigate customer behavior, discover growth opportunities, and provide data-driven recommendations.

The analysis combines product analytics, customer behavior analytics, retention analysis, and causal inference techniques to support strategic decision-making.

---

# 2. Business Problem

The e-commerce platform has experienced a significant increase in website traffic over the last period.

However:

* Traffic increased by approximately 35%
* Revenue increased by only 2%

This raises several business questions:

* Where are users dropping off in the customer journey?
* Which customer segments are underperforming?
* What factors are preventing traffic growth from translating into revenue growth?
* Which initiatives should the business prioritize to improve conversion and retention?

---

# 3. Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Build a scalable analytics workflow using modern data stack tools.
2. Analyze the end-to-end customer journey.
3. Identify key conversion bottlenecks.
4. Segment customers based on behavior and value.
5. Measure customer retention and engagement patterns.
6. Investigate potential causal factors affecting conversion.
7. Generate actionable business recommendations.
8. Create executive-level dashboards for stakeholders.

---

# 4. North Star Metric

## Successful Orders

A Successful Order is defined as a completed purchase transaction generated by a customer.

This metric was selected because it directly impacts:

* Revenue
* Customer Growth
* Retention
* Business Sustainability

---

# 5. Key Business Questions

### Funnel Analytics

* What is the conversion rate from Product View to Purchase?
* Which funnel stage has the highest drop-off rate?
* How does conversion differ across customer segments?

### Customer Analytics

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* What characteristics define retained customers?
* What is the retention pattern over time?

### Product Analytics

* Which product categories contribute most to revenue?
* Which categories have the lowest conversion rates?
* How do user behaviors vary by category?

### Causal Analytics

* Did a specific business event negatively impact conversion?
* What is the estimated causal effect on revenue?
* Which future experiments should be prioritized?
