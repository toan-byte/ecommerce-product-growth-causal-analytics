# Executive Memo: Resolving Conversion Bottlenecks & Optimizing Logistics Policy

**TO:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO)  
**FROM:** Xuan Toan, Product Data Analyst  
**DATE:** November 30, 2019  
**SUBJECT:** Mitigating Checkout Friction to Restore Q4 Revenue Growth  

---

## S - Situation (Bối cảnh)
During November 2019 (peak shopping season), the platform experienced a record-high traffic surge of **+35%** in unique sessions, driven by aggressive holiday marketing. However, overall Revenue flatlined at a mere **+2%** growth MoM.

## C - Complication (Vấn đề phát sinh)
Through an end-to-end clickstream audit (~68M raw events processed and cleaned), we identified two critical growth blockers:
1. **The Shipping Fee Shock (Checkout Friction):** Our core cash-cow category, **Electronics (70.2% revenue share)**, suffered a massive **8.20% drop in Cart-to-Purchase conversion rate** immediately following the **Nov 15 shipping fee policy change**. This structural drop-off completely wiped out the revenue gains from the +35% traffic surge.
2. **The Durable Goods Retention Trap:** Week-1 Retention collapsed to **30.9%**, and over **$119.5M in historical revenue is locked inside "Lost Customers"**. This indicates we are over-reliant on burning marketing budget for new user acquisition rather than driving repeat purchases.

## Q - Question (Câu hỏi cốt lõi)
How can we eliminate shipping friction for Electronics and reactivate high-value durable goods buyers to restore a 15% revenue growth rate?

## A - Answer & Action Plan (Giải pháp khuyến nghị)
1. **Launch EXP-001 (Dynamic Free Shipping Threshold):** Implement free shipping for Electronics orders above **$150**. This removes shipping fee shock at checkout, projected to recover **+5.0% in Electronics Conversion Rate**.
2. **Launch EXP-003 (Exit-Intent Shipping Coupons):** Trigger real-time shipping vouchers for high-value cart abandoners.
3. **Lost Customer Reactivation:** Launch structured trade-in/upgrade campaigns targeting the $119M "Lost" Electronics buyers.

## Estimated Business Impact
* Recover **+$2.4M in monthly lost revenue** by resolving the Electronics checkout bottleneck.
* Increase overall platform conversion rate from **11.95% to 14.50%**.
